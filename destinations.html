Travel Related Terms:-

Iterinary

An itinerary is a detailed plan or schedule of a trip that lists the destinations to be visited, the dates and times of travel, accommodations, activities, and other arrangements. It helps travelers stay organized and ensures that each leg of the journey is well-planned.

Layover

A layover refers to the stop at an airport between connecting flights. It occurs when a traveler has to wait at an intermediate airport before boarding another flight to reach their final destination. Layovers can be short (under 4 hours for domestic flights) or long, sometimes lasting several hours or even overnight.

All-Inclusive Package

An all-inclusive package is a type of travel deal where the price covers most or all aspects of a trip—such as flights, accommodation, meals, drinks, activities, and sometimes even excursions. It is designed to provide convenience and often better value for travelers, especially at resorts or cruise vacations.
